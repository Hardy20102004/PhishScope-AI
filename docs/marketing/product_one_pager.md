# PHOENIX Enterprise v1.0 - Product One-Pager

## The Unified AI-Powered Investigation Platform

Security teams are losing the war against digital scams. Fragmented tools, manual copy-pasting of indicators, and alert fatigue lead to a Mean Time To Respond (MTTR) that allows threat actors to succeed.

**PHOENIX is the antidote.**

PHOENIX consolidates your entire investigation workflow into a single, intuitive platform powered by a proprietary AI Copilot. Whether you are dealing with a spear-phishing email, a malicious QR code, or a fraudulent SMS, PHOENIX correlates the artifacts and generates actionable intelligence in seconds.

### Core Capabilities
- **Unified Engine**: Ingest URLs, EML files, SMS screenshots, and Crypto wallets.
- **AI Copilot**: Automatically summarize attacks, extract IOCs, and generate remediation steps via our context-aware LLM.
- **Extensive Integrations**: Natively connects with VirusTotal, URLScan, AbuseIPDB, and leading SIEMs.
- **Forensic Exports**: Generate tamper-proof PDF reports with Chain of Custody hashes for legal/HR escalation.
